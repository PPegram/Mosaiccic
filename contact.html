Before you get in touch

Give us enough
to understand.

For a research enquiry, describe the place, the barrier and the people affected. Include any relevant evidence and say whether you’re exploring an idea or have a funded brief.

For speaking or professional enquiries, include the audience, format and proposed date. Please avoid sending sensitive personal information in an initial message.

Can I request individual support?

MOSAIC’s focus is research into structural barriers. This website is not a casework, crisis or emergency service and cannot promise individual support.

Can I propose a research project?

Yes. Send an outline of the question, the community involved, the decision the research would inform and any timing or funding constraints. A conversation does not commit either party to a project.

Where can I follow updates?

Follow MOSAIC on LinkedIn for organisation updates, or Phil’s profile for his professional writing and research interests.
